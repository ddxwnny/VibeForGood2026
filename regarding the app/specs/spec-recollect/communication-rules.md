# Communication rules

Editorial law for everything a senior, a family member, or a care worker reads or hears. Applies to the weekly family window (CAP-5), the senior's weekly disclosure (CAP-6), and every device utterance.

## The governing test

**If it is not fit for her to hear about herself, it is not fit to send to anyone.** This is not a tone preference; it follows from continuous disclosure. It forces plain language, no scores, and no clinical vocabulary, because she receives the same account.

## Forbidden

- **"Stable."** An unsupportable claim about her, and a liability sentence when something was missed. Say **"nothing new noticed this month"** — what the system did, not what is true of her.
- Any word for the condition. It is never named to any user in v1.
- Scores, levels, percentages, gauges, badges, colour-coded status, trend arrows.
- Clinical vocabulary: cognition, decline, impairment, assessment, screening, baseline, risk.
- Anything phrased as a verdict about her, however gently.

## The weekly family window

A **connection surface with a safety layer**, not a warning light with a feed attached. A two-state monitor — nothing, or bad news — becomes a dread notification families stop opening, and then the safety layer lands on a dead channel.

Every window contains:

1. **Her week** — what she did, what she handled, what she mentioned. Specific and dated.
2. **One conversation opener** — a concrete thing the family could ask her about.
3. An honest note on what the system is still learning, during the baseline period.

Every window is true on day 30 with zero clinical claim attached.

## The senior's weekly disclosure

Same content, delivered by voice, in her language, within the same week. Warm, brief, and about her life rather than about the system. She is never read a summary of herself in the third person.

## Warmth

Warmth and personalisation are the **texture of delivery**, never the role. The device is a helper. It is not a companion, not a friend, and not family — the "AI son" framing is rejected outright, and no copy anywhere may imply it.

## Honesty under direct question

If she asks whether she is being tested, monitored, or reported on, she is told the truth in plain words, and reminded that she chose who is told and when. Deflection here would break the disclosure discipline the whole consent model rests on.
