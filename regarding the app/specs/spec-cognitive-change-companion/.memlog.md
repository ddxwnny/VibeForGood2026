- (capability) CAP-1 addressed-only voice interaction: wakes and captures only when the senior speaks to it; no login, no ambient listening.
- (capability) CAP-2 everyday task support: medication, appointments, official mail and letters, routines. The real job that earns the device its place.
- (capability) CAP-3 voice-native structured instrument items, delivered as conversation, drawn from rotating banks on a spaced schedule.
- (capability) CAP-4 longitudinal functional record per senior: dated task-completion series, instrument responses, and mood/sleep/appetite mentions. Record only, no scoring surfaced.
- (capability) CAP-5 weekly family window: content not verdict, plus one concrete conversation opener.
- (capability) CAP-6 continuous disclosure to the senior: she hears weekly what the family sees. No reveal because no secret.
- (capability) CAP-7 care-worker-led enrolment: Ulysses consent recorded in her own voice, named recipient physically co-signs, research/validation consent taken in the same sitting.
- (capability) CAP-8 device liveness heartbeat: silence is only clinical data if a dead unit can be ruled out.
- (capability) CAP-9 data governance: raw audio discarded after feature extraction, defined retention, access controls, withdrawal and death handling.
- (capability) CAP-10 care-worker roster view: enrolled seniors, device liveness, consent status. No ranking, no concern level, no highlighting.
