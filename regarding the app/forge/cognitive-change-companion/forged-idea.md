# Forged idea — cognitive-change companion

**What it is:** A voice companion deployed through community care to seniors living alone. It does a real job (medications, appointments, official mail), and the same data that proves it's being used is the data that shows functional decline.

## Locked

**Sensing**
- Keep structured instruments, delivered voice-native. They were originally administered aloud by a human; conversation is their native form, not a compromise.
- Signal weight sits on real-world task completion — no practice effect, and it's IADL decline, which is already a clinical signal.
- Rotate item banks, space administration. Never the same five words.
- Two-speed engine. Sharp drop over days → possible delirium → urgent, reversible, usually missed. Slow drift over months → the dementia pathway. Drop with mood/sleep/appetite → depression pathway.

**Consent**
- Capture is addressed-only. No ambient listening. No third parties recorded, no speaker-attribution corruption.
- Consent is a Ulysses contract at onboarding: she names who gets told and whether she's told first, while clearly able. At flag time the system executes that instruction. No veto — because loss of insight is a symptom of the thing being detected.
- Continuous disclosure. She hears weekly what the family sees. No reveal, because no secret.
- Onboarding consent recorded in her own voice; the named recipient physically co-signs.
- Research/validation consent taken at the same moment. Non-negotiable — retrofitting it across a senior cohort is not recoverable.

**What it optimises**
- Primary metric: task completion. Secondary: real human contact, as a counter-metric with veto — AI talk-time rising while human contact falls is a regression, even if tasks are up.
- Time-talking-to-AI is a diagnostic, never a target.
- Warmth and personalisation are the texture, not the role.

**What people see**
- The dashboard is a connection surface with a safety layer, not a warning light. A two-state monitor is a dread notification nobody opens.
- Baseline period shows content, not verdict: her week, plus one thing the family could ask her about.
- Never the word "Stable." Say "nothing new noticed" — what the system did, not what is true of her.
- Anything not fit for her to hear is not fit to send.

**Channel**
- Community care, not consumer. The care worker enrols and co-signs on a visit already happening; she reaches the no-family population, which is the target. Senior pays nothing.
- Family gets the weekly window. That's the engagement engine.
- Flags go to the care worker with their evidence attached — what changed, over what window, dated instances. Triage from a desk in two minutes, not a home visit.
- Speaker for the senior; phone for family and care worker. One capture surface only.

**Posture**
- Split product. Observation layer ships now (record, never judgement — sorting is judgement). Screening layer is regulated, later. v1's deployment is how the evidence for v2 gets made.
- v1 sells on the ageing-in-place budget line, not dementia detection. Same buyer, different line.
- Position as the who-to-test signal, not a test. If blood biomarkers get cheap, knowing who to send becomes the only bottleneck left.
- Error tuning per lane, by stigma cost. Delirium: sensitive (a false alarm says "check for infection"). Drift: specific, low-stakes wording, care worker only.

## Rejected

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| Passive speech-acoustic analysis as the signal | Research-grade, no clinical pathway consumes it, and Singapore's dialect and code-switching make the baseline unworkable |
| Ambient / always-on listening | Records non-consenting visitors, corrupts the baseline via speaker attribution, contradicts the project's own ethics |
| Asking permission when the flag fires | Asks the faculty being measured. Fails hardest on exactly the target population |
| Telling the family and not her | Nothing about her is hidden from her |
| "AI son" | Gives the real family permission to stay away, manufactures the withdrawal signal being measured, and lands badly against filial piety |
| Engagement as primary metric | Growth bought in the currency of the product's own validity |
| Ranked caseload | Cannot defend cross-person severity; a wrong ranking gives everyone below the fold a false clean bill |
| Phone as the senior's surface | Breaks the no-login lock; piloting there measures the wrong product, and a failed pilot with a government funder is unrecoverable |
| Genetics in v1 | Maximally sensitive, needs counselling, family implications she can't consent away, and says nothing about whether something changed this month |
| The word "Stable" | An unsupportable claim about her, and a liability sentence when something was missed |

## Still open

- **Cellular vs wifi**, and the per-unit cost answer for the funder. Parked mid-session. Wifi fails through household changes nobody reports; a dark unit in an empty flat goes unnoticed for weeks. Needs a device heartbeat either way — silence is only clinical data if liveness is known.
- **Cross-person prioritisation.** Cheap triage and an urgent lane only partly answer the care worker's stated pain.
- **Retention is unproven.** Genuinely unsettled. Verify against real deployment data rather than assertion.
- **Procurement clock.** Community care is the right channel and a possibly fatal timeline.
- **No true baseline at enrolment** — the population is already subtly declining by targeting premise, so day one records an already-declined floor.
- **Pitch discipline.** Intended purpose can be established in a sales meeting. The staging only holds if the pitch is staged too.
