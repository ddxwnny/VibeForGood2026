BMad · Forge Report

Cognitive-Change Companion

A voice companion for seniors living alone that does a real job — and lets the people around them notice, honestly, when something has changed.

Source VibeForGood.md · ACE Challenge Statement 2
Goal Make it better — drive open branches to concrete decisions
Context Real deployment in Singapore, not only a hackathon pitch
Session 2026-09-08 · 10 branches · 22 locks · 6 open weak points

Outcome — Hardened

The idea survived, but not in the shape it arrived in. Two contradictions inside the source document were found and resolved, one symptom the entire consent model had ignored was named, and the product’s centre of gravity moved from a companion that measures speech to a utility whose usage data is the clinical signal. It is now specific enough to build, cost and defend — with six weak points that are known rather than hidden.

What moved

Three shifts did most of the work. Almost everything else follows from them.

From speech analysis → to function

The signal is what she does, not how she talks. Missed appointments, letters she can’t handle, routines that stop.

Task completion has no practice effect, and functional decline is already a recognised clinical signal. The engagement metric and the health metric became the same quantity.

From consent-at-the-flag → to a Ulysses contract

She names who gets told, and whether she is told first, at onboarding — while clearly able. The system later executes that instruction.

Loss of insight is a symptom of what is being detected. Asking permission at flag time asks the exact faculty under measurement.

From a warning light → to a connection surface

The family opens a weekly window into her life. The safety layer rides inside a channel they already read.

A dashboard whose only states are “nothing” and “bad news” is a dread notification. The flag would have arrived on a dead channel.

Locked decisions

Addressed-only capture

No ambient listening. No login ritual, but it wakes only when she speaks to it.

Kills third-party consent and speaker-attribution corruption in one move.

Instruments stay, voice-native

The MoCA family of items was designed to be administered aloud. Conversation is their original form, not a disguise.

Two-speed engine

Sharp drop over days → possible delirium: urgent and reversible. Slow drift → the dementia pathway. Drop with mood and sleep change → depression.

Turned the biggest confound into the strongest near-term clinical win.

Task completion is the primary metric

Real human contact is a counter-metric with veto power. Time-talking-to-AI is watched, never optimised.

Community care, not consumer

The care worker enrols and co-signs on a visit that was happening anyway. The senior pays nothing.

A consumer model reaches only seniors who already have engaged family — inverting the targeting.

Flags carry their evidence

What changed, over what window, with dated instances. Triage from a desk in two minutes instead of a home visit.

The care worker’s cost was never the number of flags. It was the cost of each one.

Split regulatory posture

Observation ships first — a record, never a judgement. Screening is regulated, later. Sorting counts as judgement.

v1 makes no clinical claim, so its deployment is how v2’s evidence gets made.

Research consent at enrolment

Taken in her own voice alongside the Ulysses contract, on day one.

Retrofitting it across a cohort of seniors a year later is not recoverable.

Error tuning per lane

Delirium: sensitive — a false alarm says “check for an infection.” Drift: specific, low-stakes wording, care worker only.

Set by the stigma cost of being wrong, which differs enormously between the two.

Never the word “Stable”

Say “nothing new noticed” — what the system did, not what is true of her.

Nothing hidden from her

She hears weekly what the family sees. No reveal, because no secret.

Warmth is texture, not role

Deep personalisation stays. The “AI son” framing goes.

Rejected, and why

RejectedReason it didn’t survive
Passive speech-acoustic analysis as the signalResearch-grade, no clinical pathway consumes it, and Singapore’s dialect range and code-switching make the baseline unworkable.
Ambient, always-on listeningRecords non-consenting visitors, corrupts the baseline through speaker attribution, and contradicts the project’s own stated ethics.
Asking permission when the flag firesAnosognosia. It fails hardest on precisely the people it was built for.
Telling the family and not herRejected outright. Nothing about her is hidden from her.
The “AI son”Gives the real family permission to stay away, manufactures the social-withdrawal signal being measured, and reads as a verdict on her children.
Engagement as the primary metricGrowth purchased in the currency of the product’s own validity.

Weak points that survived scrutiny

These are not solved. They are known, which is a different and better thing.

Retention is unproven

The whole system is downstream of her using it, unprompted, for months. Genuinely unsettled — deployment evidence points both ways. Verify it; do not assert it.

Cross-person prioritisation

Cheap triage plus an urgent lane only partly answers the care worker’s stated pain. A funder would still push on it.

No true baseline at enrolment

The population is already subtly declining by targeting premise, so day one records an already-compromised floor and calls it normal.

The procurement clock

Community care is the right channel and a possibly fatal timeline. Nothing in the design shortens it.

Pitch discipline

Intended purpose can be established in a sales meeting. The staged product only protects you if the pitch is staged too — the likeliest place this plan breaks.

Device and connectivity — parked

Cellular versus wifi, and the per-unit cost answer. A dark unit in a flat nobody visits goes unnoticed for weeks. Needs a heartbeat either way: silence is only clinical data if liveness is known.
