Disposition

Survived — but not in the shape it arrived in

Two contradictions inside the source document were found and resolved, one symptom the entire consent model had ignored was named, and the product’s centre of gravity moved from a companion that measures speech to a utility whose usage data is the clinical signal. It is now specific enough to build, cost and defend — with six weak points that are known rather than hidden.

What moved

3 shifts

Almost everything else in this record follows from these three.

From speech analysis to function

The signal is what she does, not how she talks. Missed appointments, letters she can’t handle, routines that stop.

Task completion has no practice effect, and IADL decline is already clinically legible. The engagement metric and the health metric became the same quantity.

From consent-at-the-flag to a Ulysses contract

She names who gets told, and whether she is told first, at onboarding — while clearly able. The system later executes that instruction rather than negotiating.

Anosognosia. Loss of insight is a symptom of what is being detected, so asking permission at flag time asks the exact faculty under measurement.

From a warning light to a connection surface

The family opens a weekly window into her life. The safety layer rides inside a channel they already read.

A dashboard whose only states are “nothing” and “bad news” is a dread notification. The flag would have arrived on a dead channel.

Locked

Settled — not to be reopened
Sensing

Addressed-only capture

No ambient listening. No login ritual, but it wakes only when she speaks to it.

Removes third-party consent and speaker-attribution corruption in one move.

Instruments stay, delivered voice-native

Five-word recall, animal fluency, orientation, digits backwards — spoken, not tapped.

The MoCA family was designed to be administered aloud. Conversation is their original form, not a disguise.

Two-speed engine

Sharp drop over days → possible delirium: urgent and reversible. Slow drift over months → the dementia pathway. Drop with mood, sleep and appetite change → depression.

Turned the largest confound into the strongest near-term clinical win.

Rotate item banks, space administration

Never the same five words twice.

Practice effects run toward false reassurance — the dashboard reads calm exactly while decline is masked.
Consent

Consent taken at onboarding, executed at the flag

Recorded in her own voice, with the named recipient physically co-signing. No veto later, because the instruction was hers.

Mirrors the Advance Care Planning and Lasting Power of Attorney logic Singapore already trusts.

Nothing hidden from her

She hears weekly what the family sees. No reveal, because no secret.

Research consent on day one

Taken in the same sitting, in plain language. Retention, raw-audio disposal and internal access fixed at the same time.

Retrofitting consent across a cohort of seniors a year later is not recoverable.
What it optimises

Task completion is the primary metric

Medications taken, letters handled, appointments kept, routines held.

The only option where the growth metric and the health metric are one number instead of two that fight.

Real human contact is a counter-metric with veto

Time-with-AI rising while human contact falls is logged as a regression, even when tasks are up.

Warmth is texture, not role

Deep personalisation stays. The “AI son” framing goes.

What people see

Baseline period shows content, not a verdict

Her week, plus one specific thing the family could ask her about. True on day 30 with no clinical claim attached.

Never the word “Stable”

Say “nothing new noticed” — what the system did, not what is true of her.

Anything not fit for her to hear is not fit to send

The weekly note is written in language she is comfortable hearing about herself.

Channel and posture

Community care, not consumer

The care worker enrols and co-signs on a visit that was happening anyway. The senior pays nothing.

A consumer model reaches only seniors who already have engaged family — inverting the targeting.

Flags carry their evidence

What changed, over what window, with dated instances. Plus a separate urgent lane for possible delirium.

The care worker’s cost was never the number of flags. It was the cost of each one.

Split product: observation ships, screening is regulated

A record, never a judgement — and sorting counts as judgement.

v1 makes no clinical claim, so its deployment is how v2’s evidence gets made.

Sold on the ageing-in-place budget line

Same buyer, older and larger line, and it never requires saying “dementia”.

Error tuning per lane, by stigma cost

Delirium: sensitive — a false alarm says “check for an infection”. Drift: specific, low-stakes wording, care worker only.

Buys the right to be wrong often on one lane and rarely on the other.

Still open

6 weak points

These are not solved. They are known, which is a different and better thing.

Retention is unproven

The whole system is downstream of her using it, unprompted, for months. Deployment evidence points both ways. Verify it; do not assert it.

Cross-person prioritisation

Cheap triage plus an urgent lane only partly answers the care worker’s stated pain. A funder would still push on it.

No true baseline at enrolment

The population is already subtly declining by targeting premise, so day one records an already-compromised floor and calls it normal.

The procurement clock

Community care is the right channel and a possibly fatal timeline. Nothing in the design shortens it.

Pitch discipline

Intended purpose can be established in a sales meeting. The staged product only protects you if the pitch is staged too — the likeliest place this plan breaks.

Device and connectivity — parked mid-session

Cellular versus wifi, and the per-unit cost answer. A dark unit in a flat nobody visits goes unnoticed for weeks. Needs a heartbeat either way: silence is only clinical data if liveness is known.
