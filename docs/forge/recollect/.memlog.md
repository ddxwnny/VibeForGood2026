# Forge memlog: recollect

- **decision** — Session goal: make the idea better (drive branches to concrete decisions). Target context: real product/deployment in Singapore, not just hackathon pitch.
- **crack** — Doc contains a self-contradiction: Key Insight says "don't make seniors take a dementia test" and Ethics says "avoid secretly testing them", but the test battery listed is MoCA disassembled and hidden inside a chatbot. Both cannot hold.
- **decision** — Sensing is a hybrid. Embedded normed instruments (A) are the stronger gauge and carry more weight. Passive conversation analysis (B) is retained but must be made to actually work.
- **assumption** — (unexamined) That "passive conversation analysis works" is meaningful. Not yet defined what job B does or what "works" means for it.
- **direction** — User wants the product to be a conversational, human-like voice companion. Warm, no login ritual, always available. Ah Ma just talks to it.
- **crack** — User then bundled three separate things into that: (1) voice modality, (2) drop the embedded tests / "speech based only", (3) always-on ambient capture "whether she logged in or not". Only (1) is required by the direction. (2) and (3) are separate choices with large costs.
- **crack** — "Speech based only" reverses the branch-1 decision (A carries the weight) without new evidence, and lands in the quadrant that is both ethically worst (covert monitoring, contradicts own Ethics 1 & 4) and clinically weakest (Dr. Koh: no pathway consumes it).
- **note** — Key unbundling: A does not require a screen or a quiz. MoCA/ACE-III items were originally administered verbally by a human. Voice-native A is the natural form, not a compromise.
- **crack** — No-login + ambient capture creates a speaker attribution problem: helper, daughter, neighbour, TV. Misattributed speech corrupts the longitudinal baseline, and third parties in the home never consented.
- **crack** — Passive-only is self-defeating on the doc's own premise: no engagement -> no speech -> no signal AND no value delivered to the senior.
- **lock** — Capture is ADDRESSED-ONLY. No ambient/always-on listening. No login ritual, but it wakes and analyses only when Ah Ma speaks to it. Kills the third-party consent problem and the speaker-attribution problem in one move.
- **lock** — Keep the structured instruments (A). Deliver them voice-native so they feel like conversation, not a quiz. Voice is the native form of MoCA/ACE-III items, not a compromise. Passive speech analysis (how she talks) is NOT v1.
- **crack** — ANOSOGNOSIA: the word is absent from the doc. Loss of insight is a symptom of dementia, so a consent-at-the-moment model asks permission from the exact faculty being measured. It fails hardest on the target population. Some of the doc's cited "just part of getting older" / "not severe enough" responses are likely this, not stigma.
- **lock** — Consent is a Ulysses contract taken at onboarding, not a permission request at flag time. She names the recipient(s) and chooses whether she is told first / together / not at all, while clearly able. At flag time the app EXECUTES that standing instruction; there is no veto. Mirrors Advance Care Planning (AIC) / Lasting Power of Attorney logic Singapore already trusts.
- **lock** — Option 3 (tell the family, not her) is rejected outright. Nothing about her is hidden from her.
- **note** — Answer to "I never agreed": no reveal because no secret. (1) Continuous weekly disclosure of what the family sees, (2) onboarding consent recorded in her own voice and played back, never asserted, (3) named recipient physically present as co-signer at onboarding.
- **direction** — User wants deep personalisation and an "AI son" that accompanies her through hard times, as the engagement engine.
- **crack** — SUBSTITUTION / MORAL HAZARD: the product's purpose is to trigger human check-in; making the AI the relationship gives the real family permission to stay away. Success engine directly undermines success metric.
- **crack** — CONFOUND BY DESIGN: social withdrawal is one of the dementia signals being measured. A companion optimised to become her primary relationship increases withdrawal from real people, manufacturing the very signal it detects.
- **crack** — Cultural load in Singapore: "AI son" collides with filial piety. Framing a machine as her son can read as an indictment that her real children failed.
- **assumption** — (open) That personalised companionship drives retention. Marcus's pilot says no; real-world companion devices for isolated seniors report the opposite. Genuinely unsettled - worth verifying against actual deployment data rather than asserting either way.
- **lock** — Primary success metric is TASK COMPLETION (meds taken, letters handled, appointments kept, routines held). Chosen because engagement data and clinical signal are the same quantity - IADL functional decline is both the retention metric and the dementia signal. No conflict to manage.
- **lock** — Real human contact is a COUNTER-METRIC with veto: if time-with-AI rises while human contact falls, that is logged as a regression even when task completion is up. For a senior with no family, "human contact" means the care worker / SAC / neighbour, not the AI.
- **lock** — Time-talking-to-AI is watched as a diagnostic only. Never optimised. Never a success metric.
- **lock** — Warmth and deep personalisation stay, as the texture of delivery. Not the job, not the scoreboard. Drop the "AI son" framing - "helper" instead.
- **crack** — PRACTICE EFFECTS: repeated administration of recall items inflates scores. Error direction is false reassurance - dashboard reads "Stable" while decline is masked by familiarity. Most dangerous failure mode available, and it is the default behaviour of the design as written.
- **crack** — NO TRUE BASELINE: enrolment population is already subtly declining by targeting premise, so day-one capture records an already-declined floor and calls it normal.
- **crack** — ACUTE CONFOUNDS unaddressed: UTI, dehydration, new medication, poor sleep, bereavement, hearing loss, and late-life depression (pseudodementia) all mimic cognitive decline.
- **decision** — TWO-SPEED ENGINE: shape of the drop routes the response. Sharp drop over days -> possible delirium -> "see a doctor this week" (urgent, reversible, commonly missed, and nobody is there to notice for someone living alone). Slow drift over months -> the dementia pathway. Drop plus mood/sleep/appetite change -> depression pathway. Delirium detection may be the strongest near-term defensible win in the idea and was absent from the doc.
- **decision** — Mitigations: rotate item banks (never the same five words), space administration, and put the weight on real-world task completion, which has no practice effect.
- **lock** — During the ~12-week baseline the dashboard shows CONTENT, not a verdict: a weekly window into her life (what she did, what she handled, what she mentioned) plus an honest "still getting to know her" note, plus one concrete conversation opener for the family. True on day 30 with zero clinical claim.
- **lock** — The dashboard is a CONNECTION SURFACE with a safety layer, not a warning light with a feed attached. A two-state monitor (nothing / bad news) becomes a dread notification families stop opening, so the flag would land on a dead channel.
- **lock** — Never use the word "Stable". It is an unsupportable claim about her. Use "Nothing new noticed this month" - states what the system did, not what is true of her. Honest epistemics, and not a liability sentence when something was missed.
- **lock** — The weekly summary must be written so Ah Ma is comfortable hearing it about herself (follows from continuous-disclosure lock). If it is not fit for her to hear, it is not fit to send. Forces plain language, no scores, no clinical vocabulary.
- **crack** — Doc names community support workers as a target user, then designs every artefact for the family. The care worker has the home visit, the referral pathway and the budget, and appears once before vanishing.
- **lock** — Channel is COMMUNITY CARE, not consumer. Care worker enrols and co-signs the Ulysses contract during a visit already happening; funding is institutional; Ah Ma pays nothing. This reaches the no-family population that is the actual target - a consumer model systematically reaches only seniors who already have engaged family, inverting the targeting.
- **lock** — The family still gets the weekly window. That is the engagement engine and the counter-metric.
- **decision** — Accepted cost of the community-care channel: slow procurement and an evidence bar not yet cleared. Real timeline risk, possibly fatal to a startup, but it is the honest price of reaching the intended population.
- **decision** — Care worker sees PER-SENIOR FLAGS, not a ranked caseload. Chosen for epistemic honesty: the engine cannot defend cross-person severity ranking, and a wrong ranking makes everyone below the fold look safe.
- **lock** — Every flag ships with its EVIDENCE, not just a verdict: what changed, over what window, with specific dated instances. Fixes the care worker's real cost - triage from the desk in two minutes instead of a home visit to find out whether it is anything. Volume stops mattering once triage is cheap.
- **lock** — Two-tier without global ranking: sharp-drop / possible-delirium cases form a separate URGENT lane (they are genuinely comparable to each other); slow-drift flags are an unordered list with evidence attached.
- **crack** — SURVIVING WEAK POINT: cross-person prioritisation across the slow-drift population is still unsolved. Cheap triage plus an urgent lane only partly answers the care worker's stated pain. The funder would still push on this.
- **crack** — Choosing which flags to send at all IS a ranking, made silently where nobody can inspect it. Not displaying a ranking does not mean not making one.
- **decision** — Surfaces split by user: speaker for the senior (voice, addressed-only, always present); phone app for the family (weekly window) and the care worker (flag list). NOT both as the senior's capture surface.
- **crack** — SPLIT CAPTURE MANUFACTURES PHANTOM DECLINE: if she can drift between two devices, volume collapsing on one reads as reduced interaction / disengagement - a signal on the doc's own list - and flags a senior who merely changed rooms.
- **note** — Pilot trap: do not pilot on phones because they are cheap. The phone breaks the no-login lock, engagement fails for that reason, and the pilot measures the wrong product. A failed pilot with a government-linked funder is close to unrecoverable. Small N on the right hardware beats large N on the wrong hardware.
- **note** — Ops reality: wifi fails via household changes nobody reports (new router/plan) and via unplugging; in a flat where nobody visits, a dark unit goes unnoticed for weeks. Mitigations: cellular connectivity + a device heartbeat, because you cannot distinguish a dead unit from a withdrawn senior otherwise. Silence is clinical data only if liveness is known.
- **note** — PARKED by user: cellular-vs-wifi decision and its per-unit cost justification. Open.
- **note** — "Biomarker" pinned down. Strict biological biomarkers (blood/CSF/PET/genetics): NOT in the product. Digital biomarkers: yes, that is what the Cognitive Change Engine outputs - and the locked kind (task completion, functional observation, instrument scores) is the defensible one; the dropped kind (speech acoustics) is the research-grade one. Calling the output a "biomarker" is itself an intended-purpose statement and raises regulatory weight for no gain; prefer "functional observations over time".
- **decision** — Genetics (APOE) stays OUT of v1: changes management little, needs counselling, is maximally sensitive, carries family/insurance implications she cannot consent away for her children, and says nothing about whether something changed this month.
- **direction** — Strategic framing worth keeping: cheap blood-based biomarkers do not make this redundant, they make it necessary. If testing gets cheap, the only remaining bottleneck is knowing WHO to test - and the person who most needs it never walks into the clinic. Position as the who-to-test signal, not a competing test. (Verify current SG status of blood-based markers rather than asserting it.)
- **decision** — Intended purpose is a SPLIT PRODUCT. Observation layer ships first (no concern levels, no sorting, no highlighting, no medical action, condition never named). Screening layer (flags, urgent lane, evidence bundle, referral prompts) is the regulated component on a longer track. Wall test: does the layer make a judgement or only a record? Sorting is judgement.
- **note** — Elegant consequence: the unregulated v1 manufactures the longitudinal dataset the regulated v2 needs. v1 requires no clinical evidence because it makes no clinical claim - its deployment IS the evidence generation.
- **lock** — Research/validation consent must be taken at ENROLMENT, in her own voice, alongside the Ulysses contract. Collecting under an "observation" purpose and later using the corpus for medical screening validation risks a legally unusable dataset, discovered at the worst possible moment. Also fix now: retention period, discard raw audio after feature extraction, internal access controls, what happens on withdrawal or death.
- **crack** — Intended purpose can be established by HOW YOU SELL IT. Saying "in 18 months this becomes dementia screening" in a funder meeting states the purpose out loud. The staging only protects you if the pitch is staged too. Most likely place the plan actually breaks.
- **lock** — v1 sells to the SAME organisation on a DIFFERENT budget line: ageing-in-place / functional support (befriending, medication support, home monitoring), not dementia detection. Older, larger line, and it never requires saying "dementia".
- **lock** — Error tuning is PER LANE, set by the stigma cost of a false positive. Delirium lane: tune for sensitivity (false positive = "check for infection" - cheap, non-stigmatising, still surfaces real illness). Slow-drift lane: tune for specificity AND lower the stakes of the output ("worth a look next visit", to the care worker who was visiting anyway).
- **lock** — Family does NOT receive drift flags by default; they receive the weekly window. Family is told only at a high threshold and only per her own onboarding instruction. Protects her from ever learning a machine told her son she was losing her mind.
